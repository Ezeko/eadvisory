OUR VALUES IN PRACTICE

Values we live by,
not ones we aspire to.

Seven values, held in our work, our relationships and our lives. They are the foundation of everything E'lique is, and none of them outranks another. They are rooted in the scriptures that shaped our founder, and they guide how we serve clients of every background and belief.

1

Integrity

We do the right thing, especially when it's hard.

We are honest with our clients, our partners and each other, even when it is uncomfortable. Our ethics are the lens for every decision we make, not a statement on a wall.

For you
Advice you can trust, including when it isn't what you hoped to hear.
“The integrity of the upright guides them, but the unfaithful are destroyed by their duplicity.”
Proverbs 11:3
2

Ownership

We own our work, not just do it.

We take full responsibility for what we deliver. Every deadline is a commitment to a client whose business depends on it, and we treat each piece of work as if our name is on it, because it is.

For you
One accountable team that sees the work through.
“Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters.”
Colossians 3:23
3

Solutions Thinking

Curious by nature, relentless by choice.

Behind every challenge is an opportunity to change someone's story. We ask better questions, look for new paths and keep going when the obvious answer runs out.

For you
When others see a wall, we look for the way through.
“Call to me and I will answer you and tell you great and unsearchable things you do not know.”
Jeremiah 33:3
4

Relationships

Relationships are our greatest asset.

For us, relationships are not a strategy. They are our wealth. We partner with people and organisations who believe business should do good while doing well, and we invest in each relationship with care, honesty and a long view.

For you
A partner invested in your long-term success, not just the engagement.
“Two are better than one because they have a good return for their labour. If either of them falls down, one can help the other up.”
Ecclesiastes 4:9-10
5

Loyalty and Dedication

We measure loyalty in presence, not years.

We give our full commitment to every client and colleague for as long as we work together. Presence, effort and follow-through matter more than tenure.

For you
Our full attention for the whole engagement, not just the start.
“There is a time for everything, and a season for every activity under the heavens.”
Ecclesiastes 3:1
6

Excellence

Good enough is never enough.

We live in the details, because accuracy, care and thoroughness separate good work from exceptional work. Everything that carries the E'lique name carries the same standard.

For you
Deliverables you can put in front of your board, your investors or your regulator.
“Do you see someone skilled in their work? They will serve before kings; they will not serve before officials of low rank.”
Proverbs 22:29
7

Accountability

Accountability is the doorway to growth.

When things go wrong, we do not deflect. We reflect, ask what we could have done better and grow from it. It takes courage to hold the mirror up to ourselves.

For you
We tell you when something has gone wrong and what we are doing about it.
“Search me, God, and know my heart; test me and know my anxious thoughts. See if there is any offensive way in me.”
Psalm 139:23-24
