OUR VALUES

Values we live by,
not ones we aspire to.

Seven values, held in our work, in our relationships and in our lives. They are the foundation of everything E’lique is, everyone we hire, and every relationship we build.

1

Integrity

Integrity is not a value we display on a wall. It is the lens through which every decision at E’lique is made.

2

Ownership

We own our work, not just do it. At E’lique, we do not just deliver work. We take full responsibility for it.

3

Solutions Thinking

At E’lique, challenges excite us, because we know that behind every problem is an opportunity to change someone’s story.

4

Relationships

Relationships are our greatest asset. At E’lique, relationships are not a strategy. They are our wealth.

5

Loyalty and Dedication

At E’lique, we believe that people and partnerships exist for seasons. Some short, some long, all purposeful.

6

Excellence

Good enough is never enough. Everything that carries the E’lique name carries a standard with it.

7

Accountability

At E’lique, we take full responsibility for our actions, with our clients, with each other and with ourselves.
